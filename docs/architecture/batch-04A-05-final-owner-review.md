## SC-CORE-PAIRED-OVERFLOW-02 — separately verified arithmetic repair

probe executed the actual Core in **both Debug and Release**, with invariant and
sk-SK cultures. PairedProportions(1000,100,80,300) threw OverflowException in each.
Its denominator product is 400×600×380×620=56,544,000,000, which overflowed Int32
before assignment to Double. Other inputs can overflow a*d or b*c in the second
product assignment for the correlation adjustment.

those two assignments. No Wilson interval, continuity rule, phi definition,
critical-value routine, rounding, count interpretation or CI method changed.

the normal quantile uses an independent density-antiderivative series/bisection,
not Core's approximation. Small old reference cases retain their original results.
